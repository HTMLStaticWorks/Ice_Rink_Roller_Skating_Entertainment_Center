Top 5 Exercises to Improve Your Balance on Ice

Sarah Jenkins

Head Coach - May 10, 2026

Ice skating is as much a mental game as it is a physical one. However, without a strong physical foundation, your mind will be preoccupied with the fear of falling. To truly excel on the ice, you need to work on your balance off the ice.

1. Single-Leg Stands

This is the simplest yet most effective exercise. Stand on one leg for 60 seconds, focusing on keeping your hips level. For an added challenge, close your eyes or stand on a soft surface like a yoga mat.

2. Core Stabilizations

Your core is the anchor of your balance. Planks and side-planks help stabilize your torso, allowing your limbs to move more freely on the ice without compromising your center of gravity.

"Balance isn't something you find, it's something you create through consistent practice and core strength."

Keep practicing these daily, and you'll notice a massive difference the next time you step onto the Crystal Ice Rink at GlowGlide.

Our Story

Blending tradition with high-tech entertainment since 2015.

10+

Years of Experience

Where Ice Meets Neon

GlowGlide was born from a simple vision: to create a space where the classic joy of skating meets the futuristic energy of neon light and modern sound. What started as a local ice rink has evolved into a massive dual-rink entertainment complex.

We pride ourselves on maintaining the highest quality rinks—from our crystal-clear Olympic-standard ice to our high-performance maple hardwood roller floors.

Evolution

Our Journey

From a local dream to a neon-lit reality.

2015: The First Spark

Alex Thorne opens "GlowGlide Ice", a small 2,000 sq ft rink with a single disco ball and big dreams.

2018: Rolling Out

Expansion into roller skating begins. The first "Neon Night" events sell out in minutes.

2022: The Mega-Complex

Merging both rinks into our current 50,000 sq ft facility with the added Retro Arcade.

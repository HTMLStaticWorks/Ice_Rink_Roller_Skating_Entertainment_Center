Where Ice Meets Neon

GlowGlide was born from a simple vision: to create a space where the classic joy of skating meets the futuristic energy of neon light and modern sound. What started as a local ice rink has evolved into a massive dual-rink entertainment complex.

We pride ourselves on maintaining the highest quality rinks—from our crystal-clear Olympic-standard ice to our high-performance maple hardwood roller floors.
